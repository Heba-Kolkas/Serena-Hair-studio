2. What data we collect and why

Our website collects very little data. Here is exactly what happens:

    Legal basis: Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a))

  • Google Fonts - We load fonts from Google's servers. This means your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads.
    Legal basis: Legitimate interest

    Legal basis: Legitimate interest

    Vercel Privacy Policy →

3. Cookies and local storage

We use the following browser storage:

  • ss_cookie_consent - localStorage - stores your cookie preference (accepted/declined).
  • bcName - localStorage - stores the name you typed into the Black Card. Local to your device only.
  • ssVisitCount - localStorage - stores your visit count for the loyalty card tier. Local to your device only.
  • langBannerDismissed - sessionStorage - remembers that you dismissed the language suggestion banner. Cleared when you close the browser tab.

We do not use advertising cookies. We do not use Google Analytics or any tracking pixels.

You can withdraw your cookie consent at any time by clicking "Manage Cookies" in the footer of our website. This will clear your stored preference and show the consent banner again so you can make a fresh choice. You may also clear all stored data at any time through your browser settings.

4. Booking an appointment

When you book an appointment on our website, we store your name, email address, phone number, and the details of your appointment (service, stylist, date and time) in our database, hosted by Supabase. We use this information only to manage your appointment - to confirm it, to let you look it up, and to let you cancel it.

We keep booking records for our own scheduling and business record-keeping purposes. If you would like your booking data deleted, contact us at info@studioserena.no.

How "My Appointments" works: To keep booking simple, we don't require you to create a password-protected account. Instead, you look up your own appointments by entering the same email or phone number you used when booking. This means anyone who knows that email or phone number could look up or cancel that booking - the same level of access as messaging us directly on Instagram to make a change. We consider this an acceptable trade-off for a small local salon's booking data, but wanted to be upfront about it.

You can still reach us directly on Instagram (@studioserena.hair) if you'd prefer - in that case, your message and contact information is handled by Instagram/Meta. See Instagram's Privacy Policy for how Meta handles that data.

5. Your rights under GDPR

As a person in the EU/EEA, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access - You can ask what data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure - You can ask us to delete your data.
  • Right to rectification - You can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to object - You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent - You can withdraw cookie consent at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or clicking "Decline" on the cookie banner.
  • Right to lodge a complaint - You can complain to Datatilsynet (Norway's data protection authority).

To exercise your rights, contact us at: info@studioserena.no

See section 2c for what each one holds, and their privacy policies for details.
